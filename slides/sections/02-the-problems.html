knowledge from first principles

evidence matters

statements need backup if we're going to trust them.

expect others to provide evidence for their assertions, and we may call them out if we think it's lacking.

context matters

materiality: the level of accuracy we need depends on what we are trying to achieve.

a range might be enough for estimating the size of an addressable market. much more precise numbers might be needed for financial reporting.

building consensus in groups?

trust

prioritise trust and accuracy of the information that gets circulated.

access

enable decision makers to plug directly into an organisation's “central nervous system” of information.

culture

build cultural norms that reward transparency and epistemic humility.
